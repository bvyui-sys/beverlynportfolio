Self-initiated concept · Social campaign

Matcha, with intention.

An unbranded social content concept built around a simple question: how can a matcha product earn attention through useful, engaging content rather than a trend-led post?

Objective

Create a recognizable content direction that introduces the product, gives the audience something useful, and encourages meaningful interaction.

Audience

Matcha-curious people and everyday drink enthusiasts who respond to clear product information, sensory details, and relatable routines.

Creative approach

Build around three content themes: product and preparation, small everyday rituals, and community prompts. Use a calm green palette, strong type hierarchy, and repeatable layouts.

Deliverables

  • Campaign concept and content pillars
  • Feed post and carousel directions
  • Story prompts and platform adaptations
  • Consistent typography and color treatment

What to measure

Track saves, shares, meaningful comments, profile visits, and link taps. These are proposed KPIs, not reported results.

Key takeaway

Give each post a job. A product explainer should clarify; a ritual post should invite identification; a community prompt should make it easy to respond.
Speculative concept · Visual identity

Form for everyday.

A fictional movement studio identity study exploring a calm, flexible visual language that can work across social media, class information, and promotional materials.

Objective

Create a cohesive identity that feels welcoming and energetic without leaning on fitness clichés.

Audience

People looking for approachable movement classes and a studio experience that feels considered and inclusive.

Creative approach

Pair confident condensed typography with a muted green, clay, and warm-neutral palette. Keep layouts modular so the identity can adapt to different formats.

Deliverables

  • Visual direction and color palette
  • Typography and layout rules
  • Social announcement graphics
  • Promotional and informational materials

What to measure

For a live launch, review class inquiry clicks, saves on schedule posts, profile visits, and sign-up conversions. No performance data is claimed for this concept.

Key takeaway

A small set of repeatable design rules can make a brand feel consistent while leaving room for fresh content.
